Somewhere past the last bright star you can see, space stops behaving the way
you expect. Distance stops being a straight path. Time slips, stretches, and
settles into something patient. The physicists say the shape of it was set in
the first fraction of a second, and everything since has simply been
consequence. Stand still for a moment and consider that. The same silence
pressing against those distant systems is pressing, right now, against your
skin.
